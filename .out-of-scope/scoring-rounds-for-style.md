# Design rounds until a score is reached

**What:** iterating color and style until a review panel's score clears a target.

**Why not:** three rounds chased +0.5 against a measured noise of 0.67. Style is the operator's call, made with the
options side by side. Scores stay as data, and the target applies to changes of function.

**Reopens when:** the score's noise between runs is measured below the step being chased.
