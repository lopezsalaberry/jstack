# A synthetic user panel inside jstack

**What:** a skill that scores options with personas built from public quotes, used as a gate before merging UI.

**Why not:** in trials it got one backtest of three right in its first format and 4/6 only in-sample. It found
4-5 of 16 known defects. Its score is relative to the option next to it, so it cannot approve on its own.

**Reopens when:** an out-of-sample backtest predicts the direction and the main reason of a real reaction.
